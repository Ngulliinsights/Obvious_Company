## Delivery Tips

**Rhythm and Pacing**: Use the rule of three in your delivery - build tension, pause, then deliver the payoff. Notice how many introductions use this structure.

**Humor Integration**: The best humor feels natural and inclusive. Use self-deprecating jokes sparingly, and always punch up, never down.

**Energy Management**: Start with matched energy (meet the room where it is) then gradually build to where you want it to be.

**Personalization**: Each introduction can be customized with specific details about the event, company, or couple to make it feel unique and special.

**Recovery Lines**: Always have a backup plan if a joke doesn't land - a simple "tough crowd!" with a smile can reset the energy beautifully.
