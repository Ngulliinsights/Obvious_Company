# File Naming Convention Examples

## Before and After Examples

Here are examples of how files will be renamed according to the new naming convention:

### Curricula and Training Materials

| Original Filename | New Filename | Explanation |
|------------------|-------------|-------------|
| `ai_consultancy_curriculum.md` | `CURR_AI_Consultancy_CONS_v2024.md` | Curriculum for consultants with current year version |
| `ai_curriculum_intensive.md` | `CURR_AI_Leadership_Intensive_EXEC_v2024.md` | Executive-focused intensive curriculum |
| `refined_ai_curriculum.md` | `CURR_AI_Curriculum_CONS_LATEST.md` | Latest version of consultant curriculum |

## Handling Special Cases

### Duplicate Names

When duplicate names occur after applying the convention, a numeric suffix is added:

| Original Filenames | New Filenames |
|-------------------|---------------|
| `unified_framework.md` | `FRMK_Unified_CONS_v2024.md` |
| `unified_funding_framework.md` | `FRMK_Unified_CONS_v2024_1.md` |

### Draft Documents

Documents identified as drafts receive the `_DRAFT` suffix:

| Original Filename | New Filename |
|------------------|-------------|
| `strategic_framework_optimized.md` | `STRAT_Framework_Optimized_CONS_DRAFT.md` |
