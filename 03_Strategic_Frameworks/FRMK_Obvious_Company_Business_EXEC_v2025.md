# Obvious Company: Complete Business Framework & Operations Guide

## Executive Summary

Obvious Company specializes in AI leadership development and strategic consultancy, with a focused approach on the Kenyan market. The company delivers tailored AI education, prompt engineering expertise, and strategic implementation frameworks designed to create measurable competitive advantages for professionals and organizations.

---

## 1. Core Value Streams & Offerings

### A. AI Leadership Development Program

**Target Market**: 40+ professionals in Kenya seeking AI leadership capabilities

**Pricing Structure**:
- Diagnostic Assessment: 1,500 KSH
- Foundational Course: 18,000 KSH
- Executive Programs: Variable pricing (2-18 weeks)

**Curriculum Modules** (2-3 weeks each):
1. Strategic AI foundations
2. High-value AI applications  
3. Advanced strategic communication
4. Human-AI collaboration
5. Ethical AI leadership
6. Strategic implementation
7. Executive capstone projects

### B. Consultancy & Training Services

**Half-Day Intensive Workshops**:
- AI Landscape in Kenya (30 min)
- Business Leadership Fundamentals (45 min)
- Prompt Engineering Workshop (60 min)
- Implementation Planning (45 min)

**Two-Day Transformation Masterclass**:
- Comprehensive AI integration strategy
- Hands-on implementation planning
- Leadership capability development

**Executive One-on-One Leadership Series**:
- Personalized strategic guidance
- Custom framework development
- Ongoing mentorship and support

### C. Specialized Service Offerings

**Prompt Engineering Services**:
- Template design and optimization
- Testing and documentation
- Deployment and maintenance
- Performance monitoring

**Strategic Frameworks**:
- Custom AI integration roadmaps
- Readiness assessment tools
