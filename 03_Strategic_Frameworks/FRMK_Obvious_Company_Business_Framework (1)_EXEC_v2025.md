# Obvious Company: Complete Business Framework & Operations Guide

## Executive Summary

Obvious Company specializes in AI leadership development and strategic consultancy, with a focused approach on the Kenyan market. The company delivers tailored AI education, prompt engineering expertise, and strategic implementation frameworks designed to create measurable competitive advantages for professionals and organizations.

---

## 3. Solo Practitioner Service Delivery Model

### A. Streamlined Client Journey (One-Person Operation)

**Week 1: Discovery & Assessment**
- Initial 2-hour consultation call
- Send standardized diagnostic questionnaire
- Review client's business context and AI readiness
- Create personalized learning pathway (template-based)
- Send program proposal within 48 hours

**Week 2-3: Program Kickoff**
- Conduct 90-minute strategy session
- Deliver foundational AI framework (pre-built modules)
- Assign first practical exercise
- Schedule bi-weekly check-ins
- Provide access to curated resource library

**Ongoing Delivery (Batch Processing)**
- **Mondays**: Content creation and curriculum updates
- **Tuesdays-Thursdays**: Client sessions (max 3 per day)
- **Fridays**: Administrative tasks and follow-ups
- **Weekends**: Business development and self-improvement

### B. Efficient Content & Knowledge Management

**Pre-Built Asset Library**:
- 12 core presentation templates (customizable)
- 5 diagnostic assessment tools (automated scoring)
- 8 implementation frameworks (fill-in-the-blank)
- Video library of common scenarios (record once, use repeatedly)
- Email templates for all client touchpoints

**One-Person Quality Control**:
- Weekly client feedback review (15 minutes per client)
- Monthly content update cycles
- Quarterly framework refinement based on outcomes
- Annual comprehensive program review

### C. Solo Business Development Strategy

**Time-Efficient Networking**:
- LinkedIn content strategy (3 posts/week, scheduled)
- Monthly industry meetup attendance (1-2 events max)
- Quarterly speaking engagements
- Referral program with existing clients

**Automated Lead Generation**:
- Website with self-scheduling diagnostic calls
- Email sequence for warm leads (7-email series)
- Social proof collection system (testimonials/case studies)
- Simple CRM for lead tracking (Google Sheets + automation)

## 4. Solo Practitioner Systems Infrastructure

### A. Minimal-Tech, Maximum-Impact Stack

**Core Operating System** (Monthly cost: ~$150):
- **CRM**: HubSpot Free + Google Sheets hybrid
- **Scheduling**: Calendly with payment integration
- **Content Delivery**: Notion workspace for clients
- **Communication**: WhatsApp Business + Zoom
- **Payment**: M-Pesa integration + PayPal
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics + simple dashboard

**Content Management** (DIY Approach):
- **Templates**: Canva Pro for all visual materials
- **Video Content**: Loom for screen recordings
- **Document Storage**: Google Drive with client folders
- **Assessment Tools**: Google Forms with automated scoring
- **Email Automation**: Mailchimp free tier

### B. One-Person Workflow Optimization

**Daily Time Blocks**:
- **9:00-11:00 AM**: Client sessions (peak energy)
- **11:00-12:00 PM**: Follow-up tasks and emails
- **1:00-3:00 PM**: Content creation/curriculum work
- **3:00-4:00 PM**: Business development activities
- **4:00-5:00 PM**: Administrative tasks

**Weekly Batch Processing**:
- **Monday Morning**: Week planning + priority setting
- **Wednesday Afternoon**: All assessment reviews
- **Friday**: Invoicing, reporting, and client check-ins
- **Sunday**: Content planning for next week

**Client Management Limits**:
- Maximum 8 active clients at any time
- 2-week minimum between client intakes
- Standardized 4-week program cycles
- Group sessions when possible (2-3 clients)

### C. Revenue Optimization for Solo Practice

**Pricing Strategy** (Optimized for one person):
- **Quick Win Assessment**: 1,500 KSH (1 hour investment)
- **Core Program**: 18,000 KSH (8 hours over 4 weeks)
- **VIP Intensives**: 35,000 KSH (full day + 2 follow-ups)
- **Retainer Clients**: 8,000 KSH/month (2 hours monthly)

**Revenue Targets**:
- 4 assessments/month = 6,000 KSH
- 6 core programs/month = 108,000 KSH  
- 2 VIP intensives/month = 70,000 KSH
- 3 retainer clients = 24,000 KSH
- **Monthly Target**: ~208,000 KSH (~$1,400 USD)

## 5. Solo Practice Success Metrics & Reality Check

### Personal Productivity KPIs
- **Client Satisfaction**: >4.5/5 average rating
- **Time Efficiency**: Max 25 hours/week billable
- **Revenue per Hour**: Target 800+ KSH/hour
- **Client Completion Rate**: >90% program completion
- **Referral Rate**: 30% of new clients from referrals

### Sustainable Growth Indicators
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue**: 50% of total income
- **Pipeline Health**: 2-month forward booking
- **Content Reusability**: Each piece used 5+ times
- **Energy Level**: Sustainable 6-day work week
- **Learning Time**: 5 hours/week self-development

### Warning Signs to Monitor
- **Burnout Indicators**: Working >30 hours/week consistently
- **Quality Drop**: Client satisfaction below 4.0
- **Cash Flow Issues**: Less than 2 months expenses saved
- **Growth Stagnation**: Same revenue for 3+ months
- **Isolation**: No professional interaction for weeks

## 6. Solo Practitioner Risk Management

### Operational Risks
- **Single Point of Failure**: Personal health/availability
  - *Mitigation*: 2-week vacation policy, health insurance
- **Client Concentration**: Over-reliance on few clients
  - *Mitigation*: Max 30% revenue from single client
- **Skill Obsolescence**: AI field changes rapidly
  - *Mitigation*: Weekly learning schedule, industry connections

### Financial Risks  
- **Irregular Income**: Project-based revenue fluctuations
  - *Mitigation*: 3-month expense buffer, retainer clients
- **Currency Volatility**: KSH/USD exchange rate changes
  - *Mitigation*: USD savings account, local pricing focus

### Market Risks
- **Competition**: Larger firms entering market
  - *Mitigation*: Personal relationship focus, niche specialization
- **Technology Disruption**: AI tools reducing need for consulting
  - *Mitigation*: Stay ahead of trends, pivot to implementation support

## 7. Solo Practice Growth & Exit Strategy

### Phase 1: Establish (Months 1-6)
- **Goal**: 4-6 regular clients, proven frameworks
- **Focus**: Perfect delivery model, build testimonials
- **Revenue Target**: 150,000 KSH/month
- **Time Investment**: 20-25 hours/week

### Phase 2: Optimize (Months 7-18)  
- **Goal**: Streamlined processes, premium pricing
- **Focus**: Content automation, referral systems
- **Revenue Target**: 250,000 KSH/month
- **Time Investment**: 20 hours/week

### Phase 3: Scale Options (Month 18+)
**Option A - Stay Solo**: 
- Premium positioning, exclusive clients
- 300,000+ KSH/month, 15 hours/week

**Option B - Partner/Hire**:
- Bring in specialist partner
- Expand to small team model
- Multiple service lines

**Option C - Productize**:
- Create online course/certification
- License frameworks to others
- Passive income streams

### Lifestyle Integration Goals
- **Location Independence**: 80% remote capability
- **Flexible Schedule**: Client work in 4-day blocks
- **Continuous Learning**: 20% time on skill development
- **Community Impact**: Pro bono work for 2 startups/year
- **Financial Freedom**: 12-month expense buffer by Year 2

### Simple Exit Indicators
- **Ready to Scale**: 6-month waiting list for services
- **Ready to Sell**: Systematic processes, proven ROI
- **Ready to Teach**: Demand for "how you did it" content
- **Ready to Rest**: Sustainable income without constant hustle
