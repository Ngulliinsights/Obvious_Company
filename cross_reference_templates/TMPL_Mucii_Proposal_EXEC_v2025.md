## **Template Customization Notes**

### **For Different Funders**
- **Government Agencies**: Emphasize policy alignment and national development goals
- **International NGOs**: Highlight cross-border collaboration and regional impact
- **Private Foundations**: Focus on innovation and measurable social returns
- **Corporate Sponsors**: Emphasize brand alignment and marketing opportunities

### **For Different Project Types**
- **Capacity Building**: Strengthen monitoring and evaluation sections
- **Infrastructure**: Expand technical specifications and sustainability plans
- **Research**: Enhance methodology and dissemination strategies
- **Emergency Response**: Prioritize rapid deployment and flexible implementation

- [ ] All financial figures are consistent across sections
- [ ] Timeline is realistic and accounts for dependencies
- [ ] KPIs are SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- [ ] Budget justifications are clear and compelling
- [ ] Risk mitigation strategies are specific and actionable
- [ ] Sustainability plan is detailed and credible
- [ ] Team qualifications match project requirements
